## 📖 About This Recipe

Masala Maggi is India's favorite instant noodle dish, elevated with aromatic spices, fresh vegetables, and bold flavors. This quick and delicious recipe transforms simple instant noodles into a mouthwatering snack that's perfect for late-night cravings, rainy days, or anytime hunger strikes!

### 🥢 Main Ingredients
```
• 2 packets Maggi noodles (or any instant noodles)
• 2½ cups water
• 2 tablespoons oil or butter
```

### 🥗 Vegetables
```
• 1 medium onion, finely chopped
• 1 medium tomato, finely chopped
• 1 green chili, slit or chopped
• ½ capsicum (bell pepper), diced
• ¼ cup green peas (optional)
• 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
• ½ inch ginger, grated
```

### 🌶️ Spices & Seasonings
```
• ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
• ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
• ½ teaspoon red chili powder
• ½ teaspoon garam masala
• Salt to taste
• Fresh coriander leaves for garnish
• Lemon wedge for serving
```

## 👩‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/xT0xeMA62E1XIlup68/giphy.gif" alt="Cooking Process" width="400"/>
</div>

### 🔥 Step 1: Prepare the Base
Heat oil or butter in a pan on medium flame. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle for a few seconds. This releases their aromatic oils and sets the foundation for amazing flavor.

> 💡 **Chef's Tip:** Listen for the sizzle! The cumin should crackle within 10 seconds.

---

### 🧄 Step 2: Sauté Aromatics
Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and green chili. Sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add chopped onions and cook until they turn translucent and slightly golden.

---

### 🥕 Step 3: Add Vegetables
Toss in the diced tomatoes, capsicum, and green peas. Cook for 2-3 minutes until the tomatoes soften and the vegetables are slightly tender but still retain their crunch.

---

### ✨ Step 4: Spice It Up
Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, and salt. Mix well and cook for another minute to let the spices blend with the vegetables.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/l0Iyl55kTeh71nTXy/giphy.gif" alt="Spices" width="300"/>
</div>

---

### 💧 Step 5: Add Water & Noodles
Pour in 2½ cups of water and bring it to a rolling boil. Break the Maggi noodle cakes and add them to the boiling water along with the masala packets that come with the noodles.

---

### 🍝 Step 6: Cook the Noodles
Let the noodles cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally. Don't overcook – the noodles should be slightly firm and not mushy.

> ⚠️ **Important:** The perfect texture is achieved when noodles are cooked 80% - they continue cooking off-heat!

---

### 🎯 Step 7: Final Touch
Sprinkle garam masala over the noodles and give it a quick toss. The water should be mostly absorbed, leaving a slightly saucy consistency.

---

### 🌿 Step 8: Garnish & Serve
Turn off the heat. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander leaves and serve hot with a lemon wedge on the side.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Can I make this without vegetables?</strong></summary>
<br>
A: Absolutely! You can skip the vegetables and just use the spices for a simple masala version.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do I store leftover Maggi?</strong></summary>
<br>
A: Maggi is best consumed fresh. If you must store it, refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 1 day and reheat with a splash of water.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Can I use other noodles?</strong></summary>
<br>
A: Yes! Any instant noodles work well with this recipe.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Is this recipe vegan?</strong></summary>
<br>
A: Yes, if you use oil instead of butter and check that your noodle brand is vegan.
</details>
